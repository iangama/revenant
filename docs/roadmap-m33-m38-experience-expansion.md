## Purpose and authorization boundary

M31 establishes a first bounded critical-content tranche and M32 produces an
accessible, recoverable archival checkpoint. They are not the end of Revenant's
experience roadmap. The following numbered milestones extend the game through
world exploration, encounter depth, build expression, a coherent solo campaign,
replayable mastery, and final sensory polish.

The earlier authorization to execute M25-M32 does not automatically authorize
M33-M38. These milestones are now part of the planned roadmap, but implementation
was locked behind green M32 and a new explicit owner instruction. Both are now
satisfied for M33 by the owner's 2026-09-11 continuation; the retained Meridian
scope closed on 2026-09-12 in `roadmap-2026-08-30.md`. The owner's further
continuation authorized M34, completed on 2026-09-13. On 2026-09-19 the owner
authorized M35 with "Pode continuar o processo" and reaffirmed continuation.
The owner's 2026-09-19 request to evaluate and plan a larger M36/M37 authorizes
this planning revision. The subsequent selection "1" chooses the balanced
direction; "Blz, continue o projeto" authorizes the ordered continuation through
M35, expanded M36/M37 and M38 integration. Each
milestone should start with a short scope in the durable roadmap that may reduce
its provisional catalog. The owner-revised proportional validation policy in
`AGENTS.md` applies; separate per-batch contracts, audits, and exhaustive
repetitions are not mandatory.

All milestones remain solo and local. They preserve server authority, replay and
persistence integrity, frozen-V1/current-V2 compatibility unless a separately
approved protocol milestone says otherwise, original or provenance-cleared
assets, accessibility settings, and recovery guarantees. They do not imply
public hosting, live-service schedules, procedural content without deterministic
replay, another-person testing, a store release, or online matchmaking.

## M33 — World expansion and exploration identity

Objective: make Revenant feel like a place to explore rather than only a sequence
of combat rooms.

Completed as Meridian under the bounded scope and proportional validation in
the durable roadmap. The catalog below is the original planning envelope;
the closure record governs the retained content and measured limits.

The provisional bounded tranche includes:

- one new visually distinct sector connected through the existing local journey;
- three authored spaces with recognizable landmarks, traversal rhythm, safe
  orientation points, and reliable return paths;
- two exploration-led activities that reuse authoritative activity and route
  pipelines without creating a parallel quest system;
- optional discoveries, environmental storytelling, interactable details, and
  bounded secrets that reward attention without blocking progression;
- transitions, ambience, music states, lighting, navigation cues, and map/UI
  support that remain readable with M32 accessibility settings; and
- deterministic persistence/replay coverage plus frame-time, memory, loading,
  collision, and navigation validation.

Gate M33 requires the sector to improve exploration rhythm in creator dogfooding,
contain no navigation dead end or required hidden cue, and pass the complete
compatibility and recovery gate. Indicative effort: **28-52 focused hours**.

## M34 — Enemy ecology and boss encounters

Objective: create encounters that demand different decisions, positioning, and
weapon use instead of merely increasing health or damage.

The provisional bounded tranche includes:

- three genuinely distinct enemy archetypes with different tactical roles;
- two elite compositions that change encounter priorities without relying only
  on larger statistics;
- one multi-phase boss with readable tells, recovery windows, spatial mechanics,
  and an authored audiovisual identity;
- encounter compositions across existing and M33 spaces, including pressure,
  control, support, and pursuit patterns;
- explicit difficulty curves and accessibility-safe telegraphs using motion,
  sound, shape, timing, and non-color cues; and
- authoritative AI, combat, replay, bot, persistence, performance, and failure
  matrices for every retained encounter.

Gate M34 removes enemies or phases that are redundant, unreadable, unfair, or
too expensive for the measured frame-time budget. Indicative effort:
**28-56 focused hours**.

## M35 — Arsenal, build identity, and rewarding progression

Objective: let the player form recognizable playstyles and pursue meaningful
rewards without adding a grind treadmill or opaque rarity inflation.

The provisional bounded tranche includes:

- two mechanically distinct weapons that change range, rhythm, positioning, or
  resource decisions rather than acting as numerical sidegrades;
- four fixed modules with explicit synergies, tradeoffs, and server-authoritative
  limits;
- at least three validated build identities assembled from the retained weapons
  and modules, each with a useful situation and a real weakness;
- clearer loadout comparison, preview, preset, acquisition, and reward-choice
  presentation;
- authored reward arcs tied to activities and mastery instead of random scarcity,
  daily obligations, or monetized pressure; and
- complete inventory, persistence, replay, compatibility, balance, UI, audio,
  and deterministic bot coverage.

Gate M35 retains only choices that change play and remain understandable through
the in-game presentation. Indicative effort: **24-48 focused hours**.

## M36 — Cohesive solo campaign and narrative payoff

Objective: make the existing world, combat and builds support a connected journey
with discoveries, consequences and a satisfying ending. Recommended expanded
envelope: **45–90 focused hours**, replacing the earlier 30–60h proposal.

The repository already has Relay Awakening, Breach/Stabilize, Meridian survey/
recovery, the archive, tactical enemies and Prism Warden. These are reusable
ingredients, not a completed campaign. Persistent chapter orchestration,
consequences and chapter selection are new work. Meridian's existing optional
activities issue no independent rewards; campaign wrapping must preserve their
old behavior and the enclosing mission's reward ownership.

### Proposed player-facing scope

- Three acts containing **six main chapters**, with a short opening and epilogue.
  Each chapter needs a distinct objective or encounter arrangement and a reason
  to continue. Existing encounters do not count as new missions by renaming them.
- **Two optional two-step story arcs**, using discoveries and return visits to
  reveal context and open an approach, transmission or archive entry. Skipping
  both must leave a complete and understandable main story.
- **Two consequential choices**, each changing an objective, approach or encounter
  condition before rejoining the main path. At most **three short epilogue
  variants** reflect the recorded outcomes; no separate full campaign branches.
- Up to **one small authored connector/arena** where a chapter actually needs new
  geography. Other changes stage the retained spaces differently. A new region,
  enemy roster or boss system is outside this estimate.
- A chapter map, current motivation, concise transmissions, optional rereading,
  accessible reminders and a clear return to the hub. English and Portuguese
  text are included; voice acting and cinematic production are not budgeted.
- Six chapter-entry checkpoints plus safe objective boundaries where needed.
  On reconnect, restore accepted progress or restart the interrupted encounter
  from its boundary. Chapter replay and dialogue skip must explain their effect
  on progress and rewards; neither may duplicate a first-clear grant.

The following are working beats, not finalized lore or a duration promise:

| Act / chapter | Proposed experience | Reuse and new work |
| --- | --- | --- |
| I / 1 — Return signal | Establish the immediate goal and let the first loadout choice matter | Relay opening; new campaign entry and introduction |
| I / 2 — Meridian readings | Explore, connect clues and choose an approach | Survey/recovery spaces; chapter-specific objective chain |
| II / 3 — Broken supply line | Recover a resource through a staged route with a tactical obstacle | Field interactions and existing enemies; new mission sequence |
| II / 4 — Counter-signal | Reveal the reversal and make support/priority targets matter | Support and elite compositions; new encounter arrangement |
| III / 5 — The breach | Show a previous decision changing the approach to the core | Breach/Stabilize patterns; recorded consequences and transitions |
| III / 6 — Prism core | Resolve the conflict with a readable climax and epilogue | Existing Prism mechanics through a new campaign entry; ending and return |

Prism's existing optional standalone entry remains valid. The campaign adapter
must not rewrite its historical admission/reward contract. The short timed
Coolant activity is not a required campaign bottleneck; a related untimed
chapter objective needs its own authored definition.

## M37 — Replayability, challenge, and mastery

Objective: give the campaign's encounters and M35 builds distinct, optional uses
after completion. Recommended expanded envelope: **35–70 focused hours**,
replacing the earlier 22–44h proposal. Prioritize authored tactical variation;
raising enemy health alone does not create a new contract.

### Proposed finite catalog

| Family | Count | Distinct reason to play |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Combat contracts | 3 | Close positioning, distant firing lanes, support/elite target priority |
| Exploration/route contracts | 2 | Route planning and an authored recovery sequence |
| Execution contracts | 2 | Prism pattern mastery and survival/resource conservation |
| Three-stage gauntlet | 1 | Plan a build across complementary encounters with a visible recovery rule |
| **Total** | **8** | Fixed local selection with explicit objectives and rules |

- **Four modifier families**: alternate approach, authored reinforcement order,
  recovery between stages, and optional time pressure. Use at most two per
  contract, with an explicit allowed-pair list. Only modifiers that change a
  decision survive the first playable trial; no arbitrary Cartesian combinations.
- **Six mastery objectives**: one opportunity for each of the three M35 builds,
  one route-planning goal, one target-priority goal and one Prism execution goal.
  Prefer an identifiable successful attempt over kill totals or repeated farming.
- **Three simple archive badges/titles** as fixed non-power rewards. M35's combat
  equipment stays obtainable without perfect challenge scores. No new weapon or
  module tier, cosmetic asset production line, or endless progression tree.
- A contract board with rules, objective, suggested build and reward preview;
  a result screen explaining success/failure and the next actionable improvement;
  local personal records scoped to contract revision, seed and gameplay rules.
- Every contract has a completion target; time, score or clean execution are
  optional distinction goals only where they fit. Untimed exploration and all
  M32 presentation/accessibility controls remain usable. No global leaderboard.

The gauntlet is one of the eight contracts, not a second campaign or an endless
mode. Challenge runs use a separate run identity and the accepted equipped
loadout; they do not rewrite campaign checkpoints or lend permanent inventory.
Cancellation, retry and return preserve saved equipment. Only an authenticated
server outcome can set a record or grant the one-time non-power reward.

### Delivery and acceptance

| Batch | Reviewable result | Focused hours |
| --- | --- | ---: |

| M37-B | Eight contracts including the bounded gauntlet, using retained content | 12–24 |
| M37-C | Four modifier families, allowed pairs and tactical balance | 7–14 |
| M37-D | Six mastery goals, records, three badges, accessible UI and focused checks | 8–16 |

Prove one combat and one exploration contract before filling the catalog. Add
the gauntlet only after single-contract retry and completion work. Deterministic
bots validate objective rules, accepted modifier pairs and critical interrupted
runs; rendered play verifies that rules are understandable and builds behave
differently. Reuse unchanged encounter checks.

Retain a contract only if it changes route, positioning, target priority or
resource use. Remove a redundant contract or modifier before adding repetition
to reach the count. Freeze reward and record rules before final balance. There
are no daily tasks, expiring rewards, online dependencies or compulsory grind.
