This document is the operational continuation point for the current Revenant
work. It supplements, but does not replace, the milestone rules in `AGENTS.md`

## Owner scope revision — solo hobby track

After Gate C, the owner removed every dependency on other people from the
active roadmap. Revenant is developed, operated, played, and evaluated by one
person with Codex assisting in the same local workspace. There will be no
participant recruitment, sentinel session, moderated pilot, interview,
external playtest, public alpha, or launch dependency.

The completed research protocol, consent forms, observation forms, and exact
Windows package remain preserved as engineering artifacts. They are not an
active instruction to involve anyone. Automated checks, bots, two local
clients, and creator dogfooding may establish correctness, resilience,
performance, and the creator's own preference; they must not be described as
evidence of first-contact comprehension or broader player interest.

At resumption, read the current continuation point and relevant active scope,
inspect the affected files, and preserve existing work. Revisit services,
upstream state, old validators, or checkpoints only when the task depends on
them or there is a concrete problem.

## Owner process revision — 2026-09-09

The owner explicitly requested less verification and less process overhead.
The proportional validation policy in `AGENTS.md` now overrides earlier
mandatory full-gate/repetition/per-block evidence rules, including historical
milestone documents and future milestone sketches. Documentation needs text
review; small gameplay changes need focused tests and a representative flow.
Full `make check` is reserved for cross-cutting risk or packaging when existing
results do not cover the final implementation. Passing checks are reused until
a relevant change, failure, or unresolved concern warrants another run.

Use this ledger for short scope and completion notes. Do not create a new gate,
large matrix, contract, or audit for each small playable batch. Existing tests,
backups, compatibility protections, and immutable evidence remain available.

M30's additional three full 120-vector repetitions (360 rows) are removed.
Gate 6 is satisfied by reviewing the already accepted Gates 1-5, final quality
result, and documented limits; no additional runs are claimed. M30 is complete
under this revised scope, and M31 content work is the next authorized action.

### M25 — Combat pleasure and fairness

- Define testable combat-feel and fairness hypotheses as creator preferences,
  deterministic timing budgets, and server-authoritative invariants.
- Add a local combat lab that measures time-to-defeat, incoming damage,
  cooldown uptime, effective range, miss/hit clarity, and encounter duration.
- Tune time-to-defeat, incoming damage, cooldown, weapon contrast, range, and
  target clarity.
- Improve authoritative hit, miss, cooldown, telegraph, damage, and defeat
  presentation without dishonest prediction.
- Validate solo and bot-driven multiplayer difficulty, simulated latency,
  audiovisual budgets, deterministic balance fixtures, and repeatable creator
  A/B captures. Do not generalize creator preference to other players.

### M26 — Fragments, modules, and horizontal progression

- Define fragment/module taxonomy, slots, limits, compatibility, and effects.
- Keep inventory, combinations, loadouts, grants, persistence, replay, and
  migration authoritative and idempotent.
- Provide understandable comparison UI and multiple meaningful builds without
  one dominant option or uncontrolled combinatorial growth.
- Validate build arithmetic, migration, rollback, replay reconstruction, and
  local creator usability with generated inventory matrices.

### M27 — Routes, risks, events, and replayable operations

- Add authoritative route choice, risk/reward, bounded events, alternate
  objectives, and operation summaries.
- Extend the Lua boundary safely with validation, deterministic seeds where
  appropriate, authoring tools, duration limits, replay evidence, and reward
  balance.
- Use seeded bots and creator runs to compare routes; no external playtest is a
  milestone dependency.

### M28 — Local cooperation simulation

- Prototype downed/revive, contextual ping, complementary responsibilities,
  module synergy, cooperative objectives, enemy priority, and fair group
  rewards.
- Validate through two local clients and deterministic role bots.
- Define disconnect, abandonment, anti-griefing, recovery, and multiplayer
  projection rules without recruiting cooperative testers or exposing a host.

### M29 — Echo system prototype

- Write a small falsifiable product and authority specification before code.
- Prototype creation, use, lifetime, persistence, replay relationship, solo and
  bot-cooperative interaction, exploit limits, and deterministic presentation
  states in an isolated removable slice.
- Remove the prototype if creator dogfooding and measured behavior do not
  justify its complexity.

### M30 — Local identity, lobby, and security laboratory

- Complete threat modeling while retaining loopback/trusted-LAN isolation.
- Design authentication, credential recovery, TLS, lobby/invite, presence,
  versioned protocol compatibility, connection and abuse limits, secrets,
  logs, backup, monitoring, incident response, and rollback as local prototypes
  and automated adversarial fixtures.
- Treat any Protocol V3 as a separately reviewed compatibility milestone.
- Public exposure and a controlled online alpha are explicitly outside the
  active solo roadmap.

### M31 — Critical content mass

- Scale activities, enemies, bosses, weapons, modules, events, environments,
  rewards, and difficulty through validated authoring pipelines.
- Preserve asset provenance, automated content validation, replay,
  compatibility, memory, audio, frame-time, and localization budgets.
